# Project brief: Analyzing Booking Data for Splendor Hotel Groups (SHG)

As a Business Intelligence Analyst and Data Analyst at Splendor Hotel Groups (SHG), examine the past booking data of one of the group's resorts to identify trends, understand customer behaviour and recommend tactical decisions. The dataset covers bookings, guest demographics, distribution channels and financial metrics.

## Objectives

**Booking patterns**
- What is the trend in booking patterns over time, and are there seasons or months with more booking activity?
- How does lead time vary across booking channels, and is there a correlation between lead time and customer type?

**Customer behaviour**
- Which distribution channels contribute the most bookings, and how does ADR differ across them?
- Are there patterns by guests' country of origin, and how does this affect revenue?

**Cancellations**
- Which factors correlate most strongly with cancellations, and can cancellations be predicted?
- How does revenue loss from cancellations compare across customer segments and channels?

**Revenue optimisation**
- What is the overall revenue trend, and which customer segments or countries contribute most?
- Can optimal pricing strategies be identified from ADR by customer type and channel?

**Geographical analysis**
- How are guests distributed across countries, and which countries should be targeted for marketing?
- Is country of origin correlated with cancellations or extended stays?

**Operational efficiency**
- What is the average length of stay, and how does it differ by channel or customer type?
- Do check-out date patterns inform staffing and resource allocation?

**Impact of deposit types**
- How does a deposit affect cancellations and revenue?
- Are there patterns in deposit use across customer segments?

**Corporate bookings**
- What is the share of corporate bookings, and how does their ADR compare with other customers?
- Are there trends that can inform business strategy?

**Time-to-event analysis**
- How does lead time affect revenue and the likelihood of cancellation?
- Are specific lead time ranges associated with higher customer satisfaction or revenue?

**Online and offline travel agents**
- What is the revenue contribution of online versus offline travel agents?
- How do cancellation rates and revenue differ between them?

## Deliverables
- An interactive, responsive dashboard with essential metrics, KPIs and data filtering.
- Documentation of data sources, methodology and actionable insights.
- A report is acceptable if a dashboard cannot be built.

Timeline: one week from project initiation. Tools: participants' choice.
