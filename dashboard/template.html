Splendor Hotel Groups: booking performance

I analysed 119,390 bookings across the City and Resort hotels using Microsoft Excel, covering arrivals from July 2015 to August 2017. This HTML dashboard presents the findings. The KPI cards, charts, and tables respond to the filters; the written findings refer to the full dataset.

What the full dataset shows

  • Online travel agents bring 62% of bookings but 82% of lost revenue. Their cancellation rate is 43%, against 17% for direct bookings.
  • Revenue for January to August 2017 was 22% above the same months of 2016, but the cancellation rate rose from 35% to 39%.
  • Bookings made 6 months or more ahead cancel at 55% or more. Bookings made within a week cancel at under 10%.
  • Portugal supplies 41% of bookings and cancels at 57%. Guests from the UK, Germany and France cancel at 17% to 20%.
  • Corporate bookings are 6% of volume with the lowest ADR (₦71 against ₦103 overall) and cancel at 22%.
